# PixelStella — quick start

1. Keep the entire portable folder and launch `PixelStella.exe` on Windows x64.
2. Select **English** at the top right. Choose Chinese or French at any time.
3. Open **Character studio**, adjust the character and click **Set as desktop companion**. Import your own image if desired. Offline customization is not AI generation.
4. Open **Today's plans**, add a title, date and time. Categories and notes are optional. Choose one-off, daily or weekdays.
5. Keep the application running. Closing the main window leaves it in the tray. Click the pet's star or double-click the tray icon to reopen.
6. Drag the character to move it. Use **Preferences** for size, visibility, always-on-top and Quiet mode. A reminder can be postponed for five minutes from the pet.
7. In **Focus time**, choose 15, 25 or 45 minutes. Start, pause, resume or reset.
8. **Quit PixelStella** stops the app and reminders. There is no auto-start or automatic update.

Check notifications with a harmless test plan on your own device. A submitted Windows notification is not proof of delivery. Do not use this preview for safety-critical reminders.

Saves: `%APPDATA%\PixelStella`, not encrypted. Back up before repairs or moving devices. Legacy Xingban data is copied once without modifying the original. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.en.md) and [README](README.en.md) for limits and optional local AI setup.
